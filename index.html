How do I request laundry pickup?＋

Choose a laundry service and share your area, item details and preferred date. The team confirms whether pickup is available and the final arrangements on WhatsApp.

How much does a service cost?＋

Public pricing is quote-based at this stage. Send the service details you need and the team will confirm the current price before booking.

How long does a service take?＋

Turnaround depends on the service and job details. Confirm the expected timing with the team before booking.

What helps with a cleaning quote?＋

Property type, number of rooms or seats where relevant, approximate carpet area if known, location and a short description of the job.

What if I am not sure which pest I have?＋

Choose “Not sure” in the enquiry and describe what you are seeing. The website does not make treatment recommendations automatically.
