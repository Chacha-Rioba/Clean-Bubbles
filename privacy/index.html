Privacy

Simple enquiry.
Minimal data.

The website keeps your enquiry draft in the page only long enough for you to review it and choose whether to continue to WhatsApp.

Website enquiry draft

The guided enquiry composer stores what you type only in the current page session. It does not intentionally save the enquiry into localStorage, a website database or a customer account. Reloading the page clears the draft.

WhatsApp handoff

When you choose “Continue to WhatsApp”, the website opens WhatsApp with the message you reviewed. You decide whether to send it. The website cannot confirm delivery or acceptance of a booking.

Location information

The enquiry may include your area, neighbourhood and optional apartment or building name when you provide them. The website does not request exact coordinates.

Analytics and marketing

This release does not intentionally use marketing cookies or analytics to collect your enquiry text, name, phone number or address.

External services

WhatsApp and your phone service are external services. Their own terms and privacy practices apply after you leave this website or place a call.

Questions

For questions about information handling after you contact Clean Bubbles, use the WhatsApp contact button.
